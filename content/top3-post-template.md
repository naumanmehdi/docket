# Daily Top-3 Post — X Thread Template (Layer 4)

Use this once a day. Pick **3 tools** from Product Hunt / Show HN / X launches / the docket live board.
Format follows the content rulebook (SPEC §21.2): 1 editorial pick + 1 rising + 1 SKIP verdict.
**Never paid, always honest** — sponsorship buys placement, never the verdict.

---

**Post 1 (the hook)**
> Today's three AI tools — we actually tried them so you don't have to. 👇

**Post 2 (editorial pick — the one we rate highest)**
> 🥇 **{Tool name}** — {who it's for, one line}
> "{2-line honest verdict: what it does well, one specific weakness.}"
> → {url} · #{category}

**Post 3 (rising — underdog with momentum)**
> 📈 **{Tool name}** — {who it's for, one line}
> "{2-line honest verdict.}"
> → {url} · #{category}

**Post 4 (the SKIP — the trust engine)**
> ⏭️ **{Tool name}** — {who it's for}
> "{Why to skip it: e.g. hyped but the free tier is bait / doesn't deliver.}"
> → {url}

**Post 5 (close + link the board)**
> We list every tool we try on the {BRAND.name} live board — for free, and your agent can add yours in 2 minutes. → {board url}
> 1 editorial + 1 rising + 1 skip. Every day. {#top3 or #aitools}

---

### Branding rules
- Verdicts are **opinionated and specific** ("chokes on non-English audio", "free tier is real"), never a description.
- Include the miss line — a directory that only praises is an ad feed.
- Replace `{BRAND.name}` / URLs with the final brand (single swap in `packages/core/src/brand.ts`).

### Where to post
- X thread (above). Optional: one 9:16 Short/Reel from the same 3 cards (deferred to the video pipeline).
