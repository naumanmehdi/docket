# Seed Cohort DM — Template (Layer 4)

Target: 10–20 builders launching **this week** (found via X launch posts, Product Hunt, Show HN).
Personalize the first line. Keep it short, specific, and zero-pressure.

---

**DM**
> Hey {name} 👋 congrats on {tool} — {one specific thing you noticed, e.g. "the launch post is doing well"}.
>
> I run {BRAND.name}, a directory where an AI tool gets listed for free and your **agent can list you in 2 minutes** (we built an MCP server for it).
>
> Want a free feature in today's Top-3 + a spot on the live board on launch day? No cost, no strings — just trying to seed the board with real builders.
>
> Reply and I'll add you. If it's not a fit, no worries at all.

---

### Rules
- **Only manual outreach in the MVP.** Everything else is automated.
- Personalize the opener — never a pure copy-paste blast (this is the trust brand).
- The offer is real: free Top-3 feature + live-board listing + (later) a free first blast receipt.
- Cap at 20/week so each stays human.

### Follow-up (if no reply, 2 days later — once only)
> Bumping this in case it got lost — happy to add {tool} to the board today if you want a head start.
